Voting Methods in India

1. In-Person Voting (Polling Booth)

  • Most voters cast their votes at designated polling stations using Electronic Voting Machines (EVMs).
  • Voters must carry an identity proof (Voter ID, Aadhaar, Passport, etc.).
  • Voting hours are usually from 7 AM to 6 PM, but may vary.

2. Postal Ballot Voting

Available for specific categories:

  • Service Voters (Armed Forces, Government officials posted abroad)
  • People with Disabilities (PwD)
  • Senior Citizens (80+ years)
  • Essential Service Employees (like election duty staff)

Voters must apply in advance to receive and return their postal ballot by mail.

3. Proxy Voting

Allowed only for Service Voters (like armed forces personnel). A nominated proxy can vote on behalf of the service voter.

4. Home Voting (Pilot Basis)

In recent elections, the ECI has introduced home voting for elderly and disabled voters in some areas.

5. Remote Voting (Proposed)

The ECI is considering Remote Electronic Voting Machines (RVMs) to allow migrant workers to vote from locations away from their home constituency.

6. NOTA (None of the Above)

Voters can choose NOTA if they do not support any candidate. It ensures voter participation without selecting a candidate.
